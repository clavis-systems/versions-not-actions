# Data and licences

|---|---|---|
| SWE-Hero trajectories, NVIDIA (`nvidia/SWE-Hero-openhands-trajectories`; report arXiv:2604.01496) | CC BY 4.0 | A derived, filtered index of pointers (run ids, message and call numbers, categories), with no trajectory text. Aggregate counts |
